Hair color & highlights

From covering gray to a complete transformation, color at Salon DuBois is customized to your skin tone, your features and how much upkeep you want.

  • Single & double process color for all-over color, gray coverage, and regular root retouches.
  • Highlights & lowlights with full foil, partial foil, face-framing, or a quick part touch-up between visits.
  • Multidimensional color: three or more shades customized to accentuate your personal features. Our ultimate tailored color service.
  • Corrective color for color that didn't turn out the way you hoped. We'll start with a consultation and price it accordingly.

Not sure what you need? Call and we'll talk it through. A first color visit usually starts with a quick consultation so we can plan the right service.
