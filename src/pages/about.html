Alan DuBois, owner & master stylist

Over 30 years ago, Alan DuBois set out to fulfill his dream of running a premier hair studio. Today that dream is a reality you can experience.

Salon DuBois serves clients from all over Connecticut and New England. The studio is located on picturesque Bantam Road, right near the historic White Memorial. And if the charming location doesn't win you over, the smiles inside sure will.

Alan has been a professional hair stylist for over 35 years and is a lifelong student of his craft who keeps up with modern trends. Outside the salon, he's a dog lover and an avid gardener.
